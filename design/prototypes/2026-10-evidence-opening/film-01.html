Made, not generated

Everything around the film is drawn in code.

The film was animated in the studio. Its poster, thumbnail, Shorts loop and title card come from one piece of code: the robot, defined once, cut as a three-block reduction linocut in ochre, vermilion and cobalt. Change the robot, and every format follows.
